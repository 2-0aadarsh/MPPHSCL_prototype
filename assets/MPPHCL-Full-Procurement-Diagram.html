How to read the overlap: Purple nodes and the middle “Overlap zone” lane mark stages where Government and Bidder both intervene (publish ↔ clarify ↔ bid ↔ evaluate ↔ award ↔ supply ↔ pay ↔ renew). Blue-left nodes are Government-led planning; orange-left nodes are Bidder-led onboarding actions before the shared tender window.

Bidder intervention ∩ Government intervention

Government only

  • Need Identification (DVDMS)
  • Stock Check (DVDMS / e-Aushadhi)
  • Indent & consolidation
  • PR & Budget (Eoffice)
  • Tender drafting before publish
  • PO generation (e-Aushadhi)
  • Payment release authority

Overlapping interventions

  • Empanelment: Bidder KYC ↔ Gov approval
  • Pre-bid: queries ↔ corrigenda (NIC)
  • Bidding: submit ↔ open/evaluate (NIC)
  • Award: LOA ↔ acknowledge / PBG
  • Contract: agreement + Eoffice noting
  • Supply: delivery ↔ GRN / QC
  • Pay: invoice ↔ 3-way match
  • Renewal: vendor request ↔ RM finalize
  • SLA: escalation on both sides

Bidder only (until handoff)

  • Company registration pack
  • KYC document upload
  • EMD / bid security payment
  • Sealed bid preparation offline
  • Dispatch & challan creation
  • Invoice raise & bank tracking

Where MPPHCL captures data today

DVDMS (+ e-Aushadhi)

Operational supply-chain system of record inside i-MCS.

  • Need / stock / open PO
  • Automated indent signals
  • Purchase Order (e-Aushadhi)
  • GRN, batch, distribution
  • EMMS for equipment AMC context

NIC portal

State / NIC e-Procurement (GePNIC-style) for competitive bidding.

  • NIT / RFP publication
  • Pre-bid & corrigenda
  • e-Bid + EMD
  • Bid opening & evaluation pack
  • LOA / award notices

Eoffice

Digital file movement for administrative & financial decisions.

  • PR / AS / FS sanctions
  • Tender approval noting
  • Contract / legal concurrence
  • Vendor approval files
  • Payment sanction files

Manual

Paper, scanned notes, offline forms still in the loop.

  • Indent Form IND-01
  • Handwritten / scanned sanctions (OCR)
  • Physical QC / acceptance
  • PBG hard copies / SFMS proofs
  • Invoice & challan packs
